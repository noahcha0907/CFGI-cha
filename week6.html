AN EXERCISE IN BAD UX

Volume has weight.

A simple volume control. Some assembly—and continuous physical effort—required.

Volume: 35%

Press Play video, then try the volume knob.

LIFT KNOB ABOVE THIS LINE TO ADJUST
VIDEO / ALSO SUBJECT TO GRAVITY ⠿
THE FLOORGRAVITY: NON-NEGOTIABLE

01 Try to click the knob. Lose the knob. And the video.

02 Grab the knob again and lift it above the dotted line.

03 Keep holding it. Use ↑ / ↓ to change volume. Let go and it falls again.

On touch: hold the knob with one finger; tap with another.

Keyboard experiment: focus the knob, press Enter to drop it, then hold Space to lift it and use ↑ / ↓. Play / pause and reset stay within reach.
